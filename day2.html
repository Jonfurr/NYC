Timed entry booked at 911memorial.org for the 3:00 PM slot. The two outdoor reflecting pools are free and open; the museum ticket covers the underground exhibits. Budget 2–2.5 hours. 6:30 PM
Katz's Delicatessen
205 E Houston St. Take the ticket they hand you at the door and do not lose it — you pay with it on the way out, and losing it is a $50 replacement. Order pastrami on rye at the counter, tip the cutter a couple bucks and he'll hand you a warm sample slice. Cash-friendly, chaotic, legendary.
WALK ~7 min to Fulton St → J toward Brooklyn to Essex St, ~10 min → walk 8 min north on Essex/Ludlow to Houston.
