Staten Island Ferry — sunset round trip Anchor
Free, no tickets, departs every 30 min. Board on the right side heading out for the Statue of Liberty. Sunset is ~8:20 PM and hits on the return leg. ~50 min round trip; you don't get off in Staten Island — just re-board.
Honest note: this is the one fully flexible thing all week. If the 3:30 AM wake-up wrecked everyone, skipping it costs $0 and nothing needs rebooking. But it's a breezy boat with a skyline view — usually the perfect arrival-day activity.
FERRY Walk on at Whitehall. Return: 1 uptown from South Ferry → 28 St, ~25 min.
