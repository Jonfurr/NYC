The Bushwick Collective — street art
Outdoor mural gallery centered on Troutman St around St. Nicholas and Wyckoff — several blocks of rotating large-scale street art. Free, no tickets, your own pace; 45–60 min. Late morning on purpose: zero shade out here, and the light is better for photos.
WALK 1 block from the Jefferson St station exit.

East River route, southbound — skyline and both bridges from the water. $4.50, air-conditioned cabin, ~15–20 min. The boat ride done right.

DUMBO — the Washington St view
Manhattan Bridge framed between the brick warehouses, Empire State Building visible through the arch — the most photographed spot in the borough, and it earns it. Wander the cobblestones, hit a bookstore or the Time Out rooftop for the view.
