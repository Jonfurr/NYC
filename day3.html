Loose block: Ess-a-Bagel → Grand Central → NYPL
No timestamps on purpose — all three sit within a few blocks. Ess-a-Bagel (831 3rd Ave at 51st) whenever you're up, then Grand Central for the constellation ceiling and the whispering gallery, then the New York Public Library's Rose Main Reading Room with a shaded sit in Bryant Park next door. Skip any of the three if the morning runs slow.
Only rule: be walking toward 53rd St by 11:40.
WALK 7 min to 34 St–Herald Sq → M toward Queens to Lexington Av/53 St, 3-min walk to Ess-a-Bagel (~25 min total). Then all on foot: 12 min down Lexington to Grand Central, 5 min west on 42nd to the library, 12 min up 5th Ave to 53rd.

Lunch — The Modern, at MoMA Anchor
9 W 53rd St. Two Michelin stars, dining room overlooking the sculpture garden. Prix fixe lunch, business casual (a collared shirt is fine, no jacket needed). Budget ~2 hours. This is the Michelin box checked at a fraction of a tasting-menu dinner price.
Fallback: if the Dining Room has no Monday lunch table, the Bar Room next door is the same kitchen à la carte — cheaper, no dress code, walk-ins accepted.
The Modern — 9 W 53rd St

MoMA
Same building — you're already there, which is the whole point of this day. Open Mondays 10:30–5:30. Starry Night is on floor 5; go straight up, then work down. Stay until 5:00 or bail at 3:30 — your call. Download the free MoMA app beforehand for the audio guide and map; no paid tour needed.
WALK Zero — The Modern is inside the MoMA complex.
MoMA — 11 W 53rd St
7:30 PM
Top of the Rock Anchor
30 Rockefeller Plaza. Timed entry booked for sunset (8:18 PM on 7/20). Going up at 7:30 puts you on the deck for golden hour into blue hour. The view frames the Empire State Building and Central Park in one shot — the thing the Empire State deck can't give you. Between MoMA and this, there's a gap: coffee, a rest at the hotel, or a slow wander through Rockefeller Center.
WALK 3 min from MoMA — 53rd to 50th between 5th and 6th. Home after: BDFM from 47–50 Sts–Rockefeller Ctr → 34 St–Herald Sq, then 7-min walk.

Transport notes for today
On foot ★
This is the most walkable day of the trip — once you're in Midtown, Ess-a-Bagel, Grand Central, NYPL, MoMA and Top of the Rock are all within a ~15-min walking radius. Barely touch the subway after the morning.
Comfortable shoes matter · lots of standing on marble
M in, BDFM out
M train from Herald Sq gets you to the 53rd St corridor in the morning; the B/D/F/M from Rockefeller Center brings you home at night. Both are a short walk from the hotel.
$2.90 flat each way
Dinner gap
After MoMA closes (5:30) you have ~2 hrs before Top of the Rock. Easiest to grab a light bite in Midtown rather than trek home — you had the big meal at noon.
