Locals-only tips
  • Subway: tap credit card or phone at every turnstile (OMNY). Fares cap automatically at the weekly max — no math required. Stand clear of the doors, let people off first, backpack in front on crowded cars.
  • Ferry vs. subway: anytime the ferry is an option, it's the better ride — AC, seats, and the skyline for $4.50. It just runs less often, so check the app schedule before committing.
  • Walking: keep right, don't stop mid-sidewalk, cross when the locals cross. Ben will have this down by Day 2.
  • Broadway hacks: TodayTix lottery the night before, TKTS booth in Times Square for day-of discounts, $45 rush at the Jacobs box office at 10 AM.
  • Bushwick murals: totally safe and busy with photo-walkers by late morning; it's a working neighborhood, not a tourist zone — which is the appeal.
