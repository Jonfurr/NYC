Mon 7/20Midtown classics, The Modern & MoMA

Morning
Loose block: Ess-a-Bagel → Grand Central → NYPL
No timestamps on purpose — all three sit within a few blocks. Suggested flow: Ess-a-Bagel (831 3rd Ave at 51st) whenever you're up, Grand Central for the concourse ceiling and the whispering gallery, then the Rose Main Reading Room at the library with a shaded sit in Bryant Park. Skip any of the three if the morning runs slow — nothing downstream depends on them.
Only rule: be walking toward 53rd St by 11:40.
12:00 PM
Lunch — The Modern, at MoMA Anchor
9 W 53rd St, two Michelin stars, dining room overlooking the sculpture garden. Prix fixe lunch, business casual (collared shirt fine, no jacket needed). Budget ~2 hours. This is the Michelin box, checked, at a fraction of a tasting-menu dinner.
Fallback: if the Dining Room has no Monday lunch table left, the Bar Room next door is the same kitchen à la carte — cheaper, no dress code, walk-ins accepted. Less formal, still excellent.
2:15 PM
MoMA
Same building — you're already there, which is the whole point of this day. Open Mondays 10:30–5:30. Starry Night is floor 5; go straight up, then work down. Stay until 5:00 or leave at 3:30.
7:30 PM
Top of the Rock Anchor
30 Rockefeller Plaza, a 3-min walk from MoMA — the whole reason it's tonight. Timed entry booked for sunset (8:18 PM on 7/20). Going up at 7:30 puts you on the deck for golden hour into blue hour, city lights coming on as you leave. The view frames the Empire State Building and Central Park in one shot — the thing the Empire State deck can't give you.
