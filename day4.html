The Metropolitan Museum of Art
Open Tuesdays (closed Wednesdays — that's exactly why today). Arrive at open and hit your must-sees first: the Temple of Dendur, Arms & Armor (Ben's pick), European paintings. Free volunteer-led highlights tours leave from the Great Hall, first come first served — take one only if a departure lines up; don't plan around it. Hard stop at 12:15. You cannot "do" the Met and shouldn't try.
TAXI / LYFT ~15–20 min up Madison Ave (~$20–25) — the honest fastest route from a west-side hotel.
Subway option: 1 to Times Sq → S shuttle to Grand Central → 45 uptown to 86 St, 3 blocks west (~40 min). At 9:10 AM, take the taxi.

Central Park — shaded lunch & the AMNH vote
Grab something near the museum or from a cart and find shade near Turtle Pond. This is where the family votes: American Museum of Natural History, or a slow park afternoon?
